About Us

Brief History

Wheels on Fire has been founded by car collector John O'Reilly in 1985 starting on a farm in County Galway. His business ended up thriving further and further and over the years it has expanded into Dublin, Sligo, Offaly and many more.

Stock

Our stock contains a variety of vehicles from the cheapest to the most expensive offering a wide range for our customers to choose from which can be explored on this website.
